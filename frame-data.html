Understanding Frame Data

Frame data describes how long different parts of an attack take to happen. Understanding these values can help players determine when they can attack, defend, or punish an opponent.

Startup

The number of frames before the attack becomes active. Lower startup values generally mean the attack comes out faster.

Active

The frames during which the attack's hitbox is active. These are the frames where the attack can connect with an opponent.

Recovery

The frames after the active portion where the character returns toward a neutral state. Longer recovery generally gives the opponent more time to respond.

On Hit

The frame advantage or disadvantage when the attack successfully hits the opponent. Positive values generally mean the attacker recovers first.

On Block

The frame advantage or disadvantage after the opponent blocks the attack. Negative values can mean the attacker recovers later.
